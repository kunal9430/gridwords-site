How to play

Easy to learn. Hard to master.

Every turn is two quick moves: place a letter, then claim a word that runs through it.

Place a letter

Tap any empty cell, then pick a letter from the Alphabet Bank to lock it in.

Pick your word

Tap the first and last letter of your word. Across, down or diagonal, either direction.

Use your new letter

The word must pass through the letter you just placed, with no gaps between letters.

Score its length

A good word scores its length. A word can only score once per match.
